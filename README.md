# I'm Hubert Luz de Miranda

### Embedded Firmware & Linux Engineering Student | CentraleSupélec × UFC

I'm a computer engineering student on a **double-degree track between [CentraleSupélec](https://www.centralesupelec.fr/) (Gif-sur-Yvette, France) and the [Federal University of Ceará (UFC)](https://ufc.br/) (Brazil)**, working mostly at the low level, where hardware meets software.

I'm looking for hands-on experience in **embedded systems**, especially **autonomous and connected vehicles**.

---

### 🔬 What I've been working on

Most of my experience comes from Embrapii industry-partnership projects at **LESC** (Computer Systems Engineering Lab, UFC):

- **Vision board bring-up (Intern, 01/2025 – 05/2026)** – firmware for a dedicated computer-vision board inside a drone's onboard computer (Quectel SG882G / Qualcomm QCS8550 module). I validated the custom board's interfaces and worked on the camera stack (Sony IMX577, CamX/Chi-CDK on Yocto) and on SPI bring-up under embedded Linux.
- **Atletec (Research scholar, 01/2026 – 08/2026)** – UWB firmware on Qorvo SDK / nRF52840 hardware, including porting a BQ27427 fuel-gauge driver from Zephyr to bare metal through a small HAL built on `nrfx_twim`.
- **BLE smart dynamometer (Research scholar, since 08/2025)** – STM32 firmware: load-cell acquisition code, filtering to avoid spikes and noise, and persistence of key variables in flash.

### 🎓 Beyond the lab

- Member of **PET Engenharia de Computação (UFC)** from 2023 to 2025 and **General Coordinator of SEELECT** in 2024.
- Team project **[CNN_ESP_FIRE](https://github.com/hubertlm/CNN_ESP_FIRE)**: on-device fire detection with an INT8 CNN on the ESP32-CAM, where I was responsible for the low-level firmware (ESP-IDF).
- Coursework projects in digital design ([SPI controller in SystemVerilog](https://github.com/hubertlm/Projeto_Final_SEDR)), databases ([football management system](https://github.com/hubertlm/App_Futebol_DB)) and pattern recognition ([face recognition](https://github.com/hubertlm/TC02)).
